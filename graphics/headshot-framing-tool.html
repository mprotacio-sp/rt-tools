    TRANSFORM

    1. Zoom

    Roughly center the image by dragging. Zoom to fit the face to the silhouette outline.

    2. Rotate

    When rotating, if the background contains strong vertical or horizontal lines, try to keep them vertical or horizontal rather than straightening the subject's face.

    Reposition the image by dragging with the mouse, or use the arrow keys to nudge it one pixel at a time.

    CORRECTIONS

    If the photo edge shows inside the circular frame, choose a neutral color or use the eyedropper to sample the photo background. This color fills transparent areas behind the photo.
